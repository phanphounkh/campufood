# Seed media sources

All photos are from Pexels or Unsplash. Both licenses allow free commercial use and do not require attribution. Credits are recorded here anyway.

- Pexels License: https://www.pexels.com/license/

Processing: food photos were center-cropped to 4:3, resized to 800x600, stripped of metadata and saved as JPEG at quality 82. The one exception is `banana-coconut-milk`, which was cropped from the bottom so the bowl is not cut off. The hero was cropped to 5:4 and resized to 1200x960.

Files: `backend/seed_media/foods/<slug>.jpg` and `frontend/static/images/hero.jpg`.

`frontend/static/images/cta-poster.jpg` (the home page call-to-action background) is a 1600x500 collage built from five of the photos below: beef-skewers, noodle-soup, fried-noodles, iced-milk-tea and mango-sticky-rice, each center-cropped to a portrait tile with 8px brand-purple gutters, JPEG quality 80.
